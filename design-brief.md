# KOPI TA Design Brief

Design read: final-semester students and coffee learners, addressed with quiet documentary confidence.
Concept spine: the page is a field journal moving from source to cup, then from draft to final page.
Delivery tier: cinema
Animation mode: animated-website
Journey shape: single-shot, using the existing 9-second coffee process film as a scroll-controlled visual layer.
Journey: Hulu (coffee garden), Panen (hands and learning), Proses (selection and handling), Roast (transformation), Racik (product), Hilir (student work desk).
Camera architecture: A, one continuous forward visual treatment with semantic HTML chapters over the media.
Seam direction: none, single-shot. The film resolves from a coffee preparation establishing frame into a darker finished visual state.
Mobile framing: center-safe crop, portrait sections use real documentation photos and the film is hidden under reduced motion.
Locked palette: bone paper #e9e7de, deep ink #171917, forest #214c3b, muted clay #bc755e. These are drawn from coffee foliage, printed paper, and the existing KOPI TA identity.
Locked type: Georgia as a restrained editorial display face with system sans for notes and ordering controls.
Hero architecture: dark full-bleed film with left-aligned oversized chapter title.
Section system: alternating field-note layouts, dark human portrait, process index, product catalog, and manifesto.
CTA inventory: trace journey, order now, and add to cart each keep separate interaction identities.
Asset plan: existing KOPI TA logo and sprite assets; six selected documentation photos for origin, people, process, roast, brew, and hilir; existing hero video; no factual farm metadata invented.
Principle: the product appears only after the visitor understands the work behind it.
